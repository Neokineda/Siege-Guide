How a round works

Each round is short and has a clear order of events.

  1. Prep

    Attackers send drones to scout the building and find the objective. Defenders reinforce walls, set up barricades and place gadgets.

  2. Action

    Attackers breach in and try to complete the objective before the timer ends. Defenders hold them off.

  3. Result

    A round ends when the objective is won, a team is eliminated, or time runs out. Teams swap sides during the match.

Attack and defense

The two sides play differently, which is what makes each map worth learning.

Attackers

Choose a spawn, scout with drones, then push in together.

  • Breach walls, hatches and barricades
  • Use drones to find enemies before entering
  • Disable defender gadgets and trade kills

Defenders

Pick a room to protect and build it up during prep.

  • Reinforce walls so attackers can’t shoot through
  • Place cameras, traps and barricades
  • Rotate to cover the objective when under pressure

Game modes

Hostage and Secure Area are in Quick Match, while Ranked and Unranked play Bomb only. Short-lived arcade playlists rotate in each season, so check the in-game menu for what’s on now. Dual Front, the 6v6 mode added with Siege X in June 2025, was removed in June 2026.

Bomb

Attackers find and defuse a bomb. Defenders stop them. The only mode in Ranked and Unranked.

Secure Area

Attackers capture a point. Defenders keep control of it until time runs out.

Hostage

Attackers rescue a hostage and bring them out. Defenders keep the hostage inside.

Arcade playlists

Limited-time modes, such as 1v1 and 3v3 duels, appear for a few weeks at a time.

    Tips for new players

    • Learn a few maps well before trying to learn every operator.
    • Use your drone on attack. Information beats guessing.
    • Play together and call out enemy positions. Communication wins rounds.
    • Aim at head height and check angles slowly. Rushing gets you killed.
    • Start with a starter operator, such as Sledge on attack or Mute on defense, then branch out.
    • Try Unranked or Quick Match to learn the game without rank pressure.
